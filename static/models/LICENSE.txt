Spacecraft models in this folder, credit NASA except JUICE (ESA):

voyager.glb         "Voyager Probe (A)", used for both Voyager 1 and Voyager 2
parker.glb          "Parker Solar Probe"
  From NASA 3D Resources (https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources), which describes its assets
  as "free and without copyright". Files are unchanged from the source.

new-horizons.glb    "New Horizons 3D Model", https://science.nasa.gov/resource/new-horizons-3d-model/
europa-clipper.glb  "Europa Clipper 3D Model", https://science.nasa.gov/resource/europa-clipper-3d-model/
  Credit: NASA Visualization Technology Applications and Development (VTAD). Downloaded from NASA
  Science's 3D resources and made smaller for the web with glTF Transform (Draco-compressed
  geometry, WebP textures at most 1024 px, meshes simplified to about half): 3.3 MB to 0.19 MB and
  35.7 MB to 1.3 MB.

juice.glb           JUICE, credit ESA SPICE Service. Assembled for this site from the Cosmographia
  model parts ESA's SPICE Service publishes (https://spiftp.esac.esa.int/data/SPICE/JUICE/misc/models/:
  bus v07, solar array v02, MGA dish and pointing mechanism v04), each placed with ESA's JUICE SPICE
  kernels; the magnetometer boom and radio antennas are left out. Script: scripts/assemble-juice.mjs.
  Then made smaller like the two above: 7.1 MB to 0.35 MB.
  No licence is stated for these parts. They are used here, with credit, on the assumption that ESA
  allows this for a non-commercial educational site; ask ESA (the JUICE SPICE contact is named in
  misc/cosmo/aareadme.txt there) before any other use.

Use of the NASA models follows NASA's media guidelines (https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media); no
NASA endorsement is implied.
